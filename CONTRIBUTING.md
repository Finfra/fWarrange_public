# Contributing

Thanks for your interest in improving this project. Issues and pull requests are welcome.

## Contributor License Agreement (CLA)

**External pull requests are accepted only after you sign the
[Finfra CLA](https://cla-assistant.io/Finfra/fWarrange_public) electronically.** The CLA
Assistant bot comments on your first pull request with a link; signing takes one click
with your GitHub account and covers your later pull requests too.

The CLA lets Finfra offer the project under its open-source license and, separately,
under commercial licenses. You keep the copyright in your contribution, and Finfra
promises to keep it available under the open-source license in effect when you
contributed.

> 한국어: 외부 풀 리퀘스트는 [Finfra CLA](https://cla-assistant.io/Finfra/fWarrange_public) 에 전자 서명한 뒤에만 받아들입니다. 대한민국 거주 개인에게는 한국어본이 동등한 효력을 가집니다.
